# E-Commerce Sales Analysis – SQL

## About the Project

This project focuses on analyzing e-commerce sales data using MySQL.

The analysis looks at sales, profit, products, customers, cities, payment methods, discounts, customer demographics, and monthly sales trends. SQL queries were used to explore the data and find useful business insights.

## Project Objectives

The main objectives of this project are to:

- Analyze overall sales and profit
- Compare sales and profit across product categories
- Analyze performance across different cities
- Compare payment methods
- Identify top-selling products and customers
- Analyze customer demographics
- Understand the effect of discounts on profit
- Analyze monthly sales and profit trends
- Practice SQL techniques used in real-world data analysis

## Dataset

The dataset contains 100 e-commerce orders with 15 columns, including:

- Order ID
- Order Date
- Customer ID
- Customer Name
- Gender
- Age
- City
- Category
- Product Name
- Quantity
- Unit Price
- Sales
- Discount
- Profit
- Payment Method

## SQL Analysis

### Overall Performance

Calculated total sales, total profit, total orders, average order value, average profit, discounts, unit price, and profit margin.

### Category Analysis

Compared sales, profit, and profit margin across different product categories.

### City Analysis

Analyzed sales, profit, and profit margin across different cities.

### Payment Method Analysis

Compared sales, profit, and profit margin for different payment methods.

### Product Analysis

Analyzed product-level sales and profit and identified the top 5 products based on sales.

### Customer Analysis

Identified customers with the highest sales and profit.

### Customer Demographics

Compared sales and profit between male and female customers and analyzed average customer age.

### Discount Analysis

Grouped orders into different discount levels and analyzed how discounts affected profitability.

### Monthly Sales Trend

Analyzed monthly sales and profit trends throughout 2025.

## Key Insights

Some of the main findings from the analysis are:

- Electronics generated the highest category sales.
- Mangalore generated the highest city sales.
- Chennai had the highest city-level profit margin.
- UPI generated the highest sales among payment methods.
- Laptop had the highest product sales.
- Ramesh was the highest-selling customer.
- Higher discount levels were generally associated with lower profit margins.
- May recorded the highest monthly sales and profit.
