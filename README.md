\# E-Commerce Sales Analysis — SQL



\## Project Overview



This project analyzes e-commerce transaction data using MySQL to evaluate sales performance, profitability, customer behavior, product performance, city performance, payment methods, discounts, and monthly sales trends.



\## Business Objectives



\* Analyze overall sales and profitability.

\* Identify high-performing product categories.

\* Compare city-level sales and profit.

\* Analyze payment-method performance.

\* Identify top-performing products and customers.

\* Analyze customer demographics.

\* Understand the relationship between discounts and profitability.

\* Identify monthly sales and profit trends.

\* Apply advanced SQL techniques to solve business problems.



\## Dataset



The dataset contains 100 e-commerce orders and 15 columns:



\* Order ID

\* Order Date

\* Customer ID

\* Customer Name

\* Gender

\* Age

\* City

\* Category

\* Product Name

\* Quantity

\* Unit Price

\* Sales

\* Discount

\* Profit

\* Payment Method

\## SQL Analysis Performed



\### Overall Performance



Calculated total sales, total profit, order quantity, average order value, average profit, discounts, unit price, and profit margin.



\### Category Analysis



Analyzed sales, profit, and profit margin by product category.



\### City Analysis



Compared sales, profit, and profit margin across cities.



\### Payment Method Analysis



Analyzed sales, profit, and profit margin by payment method.



\### Product Analysis



Analyzed product-level sales, profit, and profit margin and identified the top 5 products by sales.



\### Customer Analysis



Identified the top customers based on sales and profit.



\### Customer Demographics



Compared sales and profit between male and female customers and calculated average customer age.



\### Discount Analysis



Grouped discounts into No Discount, Low Discount, Medium Discount, and High Discount levels to evaluate their effect on profitability.



\### Monthly Sales Trend



Analyzed monthly sales and profit throughout 2025.

\## Key Business Insights



\* Electronics generated the highest category sales.

\* Mangalore generated the highest city sales.

\* Chennai had the highest city-level profit margin.

\* UPI generated the highest sales among payment methods.

\* Laptop generated the highest product sales.

\* Ramesh was the highest-selling customer.

\* High discount levels were associated with lower profit margins.

\* May recorded the highest monthly sales and profit.
